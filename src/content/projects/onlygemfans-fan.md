## Overview

Have you ever been gallavanting in sweltering Singapore and realized that your
pathetic $100 JisuLife Ultra2 still won't cut it? Well, boy do we have the solution
for you! Introducing the OnlyGemfans Handheld Electric Fan by WERDNAAA.

## Features

- Cool down in seconds with over 800 grams of thrust
- Dynamically adjust the fan speed with control knob
- Charge your phone with the inbuilt 5V2A USB outlet
- Illuminate your path with the 12V LED floodlights
- At least three hours of runtime at 25% power mode
- Built in battery life indicator (voltage display)
- 3-position switch: toggle to external XT60 power
