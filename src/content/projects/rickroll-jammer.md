## Overview

This VTX Jammer is guaranteed to ensure that the pilots on the receiving end are no strangers to love. With its long-lasting 5000mAh 2S Li-Ion pack, and the ability to both change VTX channel and activate/deactivate it remotely via ELRS, it is sure to wreck havoc at any drone event or funfly session. It works by having the Raspberry Pi Zero 2W output looping composite video upon bootup into the GEPRC VTX.

## Features

- Completely hidden inside a standard Tiny Whoop box
- Inbuilt 2S battery with balance charging via USB-C PD
- Change its output VTX channel via ELRS
- Endless playback of Rick Astley rickrolling video
- Broadcast at up to 2.5W with a directional antenna
- Guaranteed to cause emotional damage at a drone race
