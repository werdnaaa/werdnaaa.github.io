## Overview

This is a simple kit to turn your 75mm whoop with 0802/1102 motors and 40mm
props into a brushless FPV hovercraft powered by any 450mah 1S battery. This is
the same concept as all Tiny Whoovers previously made, but adapts the frame to
fit modern brushless electronics in 2025. (I made this because I really wanted a
tiny whoover, but I didn't want to do it with shitty brushed motors and a
Silverware board from like 2017). For more information and to download the STL
and HEX files, please visit my Grabcad page
[here](https://grabcad.com/library/75mm-brushless-whoop-hovercraft-1)!

## Features

- 3D print just 4 pieces with no supports
- Direct parts transfer from a 75mm whoop
- Plastic bag skirt for easy replacement
- FPV camera and VTX for FPV drifting
- Fully programmable with custom Betaflight HEX
